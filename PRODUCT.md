# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Candor serves students, professors, founders, managers, and office teams who record lectures or meetings because they need accurate recall without sending sensitive conversations to a cloud service. They use the app during live classes, calls, reviews, planning sessions, and follow-up work.

## Product Purpose

Candor records conversations, transcribes them, extracts decisions and tasks, and keeps the resulting memory searchable on the user's local system. Success means a user can press record with confidence, find the important moment later, and trust that the meeting stayed on their machine.

## Brand Personality

Private, precise, composed, and warm. Candor should feel like a focused local
meeting workspace with intelligence built in, not a generic AI assistant or a
technical diagnostics console.

## Anti-references

Avoid parchment, legal scales, court imagery, generic SaaS card grids, AI-purple
gradients, cloud-first imagery, bot mascots, decorative AI sparkles, and social
visuals that make private work feel unserious. Warm cream is the approved app
surface, but it must read as a modern operational workspace rather than paper.

## Design Principles

- Make local custody visible on every recording surface.
- Let the waveform and timeline become the signature visual object.
- Use hairlines, rails, and docks instead of repeated cards.
- Keep the daily workflow fast, legible, and predictable.
- Add cultural edge through motion, layout, and brand moments, not clutter.
- Make `Record -> Review -> Export` obvious without exposing model or vault
  terminology in the normal workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support light and dark themes, clear focus states, keyboard operation, readable transcripts, reduced-motion preferences, and local-only privacy language that does not rely on color alone.
