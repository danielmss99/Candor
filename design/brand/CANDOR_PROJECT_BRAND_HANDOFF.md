# Candor Project Brand Handoff

## Approved Identity

**Direction:** Keep Tab / Soft Signal

The approved mark combines a soft open C, a pointed Keep Tab, Warm Black,
Document Cream, and Candor Coral. The pointed tab is essential. It must not be
replaced by a dot, recording light, notification badge, capsule, square, or
standalone bookmark.

## Source Of Truth

Use this order when files conflict:

1. This handoff.
2. `assets/icons/candor-app-icon-master.svg`.
3. GUI token definitions in `v3/renderer/src/tokens.css`.
4. `design/figma/style-guide.md`.
5. Earlier exploration files.

## Brand Colors

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Warm Black | `#161616` | Primary dark field and icon background |
| Document Cream | `#FFF9EE` | Main logo fill and warm light surface |
| Candor Coral | `#FF6B5E` | Keep Tab and limited brand accent |

Candor Coral is not the recording, error, link, alert, or universal selected
state color. Recording uses `#C93434` and must include an icon, label, and elapsed
time while active.

## Production Assets

- Canonical SVG: `assets/icons/candor-app-icon-master.svg`.
- Raster app icons: `assets/icons/candor-app-icon-{size}.png`.
- Windows package icon: `assets/platform/candor.ico`.
- macOS package icon: `assets/platform/candor.icns`.
- Linux package icons: `build/icons/{size}x{size}.png`.
- In-app mark: `v3/renderer/public/candor-mark.png`.

All production raster and platform assets are generated by
`scripts/generate-v3-icons.mjs`. Horizontal and monochrome lockups are not used
by the desktop product and are not synthesized from an unapproved type
treatment.

## Usage Rules

- Maintain clear space around the app icon equal to at least one Keep Tab width.
- Minimum app icon size is 16 by 16 pixels.
- Use the mark on Warm Black, Document Cream, white, or quiet neutral surfaces.
- Keep the full icon field on photographic or visually complex backgrounds.
- Never stretch, skew, rotate, crop, distort, recolor, shadow, glow, bevel, or
  texture the mark.
- Never use the Keep Tab as a recording or notification indicator.
- Do not ship exploratory or pre-adjustment logo files.

## Product Direction

Candor is a calm, dependable desktop instrument. Use warm neutral surfaces,
clear hierarchy, compact controls, readable transcripts and notes, visible local
save and export status, restrained motion, thin dividers, deliberate spacing,
and direct language. Avoid floating-card dashboards, nested cards, gradients,
neon accents, chatbot personality, sparkle effects, and generic AI imagery.

## Required QA

- Verify the packaged Windows and macOS icons come from the approved master.
- Verify the icon remains square, uncropped, and legible at 16 pixels.
- Verify the Keep Tab remains pointed and coral.
- Verify the packaged executable icon matches the generated 32 pixel source.
- Verify recording does not use Candor Coral.
- Verify focus remains visible and semantic states include non-color signals.
- Verify production component colors come from shared tokens.
- Test desktop workflows at 100, 125, 150, and 200 percent OS scaling.
