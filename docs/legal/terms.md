## Use of Candor

Candor is desktop software for recording, transcribing, organizing, and reviewing meeting notes.

## Recording consent

You are responsible for following all laws, workplace policies, and meeting rules that apply to recording audio. Get consent from participants when required.

## Local data and backups

Candor stores meeting data locally on your device and in folders you choose. You are responsible for backing up files you want to keep.

## Optional network features

License activation and manual update checks may contact separately disclosed third-party infrastructure only when those features are used. Recording, transcription, meeting summaries, and local export do not require those services.

## Accuracy

Transcripts, summaries, and action items may contain errors. Review important notes before relying on them.
